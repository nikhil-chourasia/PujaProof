
**PujaProof** is a mobile-first crowdfunding and financial transparency platform designed specifically for community-organized Durga Puja celebrations. Every year, Puja committees collect money from local residents, devotees, sponsors, and visitors to fund pandal construction, idols, lighting, security, cultural programs, food, and other expenses, but contributors often have very limited visibility into how the collected money is ultimately used. PujaProof aims to solve this trust gap by combining familiar Indian UPI payments with blockchain-based transparency. A Puja committee can create a verified campaign with a fundraising target, spending categories, sponsors, vendors, and supporter benefits. Contributors can donate directly through UPI using UPI intent or QR-based payment, allowing them to pay from the UPI apps they already use without needing cryptocurrency or a blockchain wallet. UPI is designed to support QR and intent-based payments, making it a natural payment layer for the application.

The blockchain is used as the **trust and verification layer rather than as a replacement for UPI**. After a successful UPI contribution is confirmed, PujaProof generates a verifiable record containing the campaign, contribution amount, timestamp, and a cryptographic reference to the payment while avoiding the storage of sensitive payment information on-chain. Similarly, when the committee creates an expense, the expense details, invoice hash, vendor information, approvals, and payment proof can be recorded or anchored on-chain. This creates a tamper-evident financial trail that allows contributors to independently verify the campaign's fundraising and spending activity. The public dashboard can show how much has been raised, how much has been spent, the remaining balance, the number of verified expenses, and the vendors or categories receiving funds. The goal is not to claim that UPI itself is an on-chain payment system, but to connect India's familiar payment infrastructure with a blockchain-based accountability layer.

To reduce the risk of misuse of collected funds, PujaProof will include a **smart-contract-controlled treasury and multi-party approval system**. Instead of allowing a single committee member to authorize a large payment, configurable approvals can be required from multiple authorized members such as the treasurer, secretary, and committee head. For example, a ₹50,000 lighting expense could require two of three authorized members to approve it before the payment is released or marked as verified. Each approved expense can contain a category, amount, vendor, invoice hash, approval history, timestamp, and payment reference. This creates a transparent “proof of spend” mechanism where the public can verify not only that a committee claims to have spent money, but also that the expense went through the defined approval process and corresponds to a recorded payment.

PujaProof will also introduce **digital supporter and VIP passes** as an incentive for community participation. Depending on the contribution level or sponsorship package defined by a Puja committee, contributors can receive digital benefits such as supporter badges, priority-darshan passes, sponsor recognition, or limited VIP passes. These passes can be represented as verifiable blockchain credentials and displayed inside the mobile application with a unique QR code. At the Puja venue, organizers can scan the QR code and verify whether the pass is authentic, valid, unused, or revoked. This creates a practical use of blockchain beyond fundraising: the same system that provides financial transparency can also provide verifiable digital access credentials.

The platform can additionally support **verified sponsors and vendors**. Local businesses can sponsor a Puja through the platform, while committees can register vendors for services such as decoration, lighting, sound, security, and food. Payments and expenses associated with these vendors can become part of the public financial record. A sponsor dashboard can show the amount contributed and the benefits received, while the public campaign page can show aggregated funding sources such as community contributions, sponsorships, and pass sales. This creates a complete digital ecosystem around a Puja rather than limiting the product to a simple donation form.

As a secondary feature, PujaProof will include an **AI-powered voice assistant using ElevenLabs** to make the platform easier and more engaging to use. The voice assistant will not control the core financial system autonomously; instead, it will act as a conversational interface over verified application and blockchain data. A user could ask, “How much has this Puja collected?”, “How much has been spent on lighting?”, “Show me the verified expenses,” or “What benefits do I get from my contribution?” The assistant can retrieve the relevant data and respond conversationally. It could also guide a user through making a contribution before handing the actual payment over to the normal UPI flow. This makes voice a useful accessibility and engagement layer while keeping the financial logic and transaction authorization under the application's controlled payment and smart-contract systems.

The core user journey is therefore simple: a user discovers a Puja campaign inside the mobile application, selects an amount, completes the contribution through UPI, receives confirmation, and gets a verifiable contribution record and eligible digital benefits. The committee manages its treasury through the organizer dashboard, creates expenses, obtains the required approvals, and records payment evidence. Contributors and the public can then open the transparency dashboard and independently inspect the campaign's financial activity. The complete system connects **UPI for real-world payments, smart contracts for programmable treasury rules, blockchain for verifiable records, QR codes for physical-world pass verification, and ElevenLabs for conversational assistance**.

The central idea behind PujaProof is therefore simple: **people should not have to blindly trust a Puja committee with their contributions. They should be able to contribute easily through UPI and independently verify how the community's money is managed.** By combining India's familiar digital payment infrastructure with blockchain-based proof of spending and verifiable digital passes, PujaProof transforms community fundraising into a more transparent, accountable, and engaging experience.@Canva
